Mosaic — for testers
====================

You need: an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14 or newer. Nothing else.
Python and everything Mosaic needs is inside the app. No Terminal needed
unless something goes wrong (see below).

Install
-------
1. Open Mosaic.dmg and drag Mosaic.app into Applications.
2. Open Mosaic from Applications. The first time, right-click it and
   choose Open (the app is not notarized yet, so double-click is blocked).
3. A setup window appears. Everything below happens there — no browser.

Onboard (2 minutes)
-------------------
1. Paste your Gemini API key and press Save & test. Green tick means it
   works. Get a key free at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
2. Scan the QR code: WhatsApp on your phone → Settings → Linked Devices
   → Link a device → point at the screen.
3. Send yourself any WhatsApp message (your own chat). The window confirms
   your Me chat on its own.

Use it
------
Type /help in any WhatsApp group. The answer lands in your Me chat —
nothing is ever posted into groups. Commands: /help /pending /stats
/recap /eli5 /sotu.

Your data (key, pairing, history) lives only on your Mac, in
~/Library/Application Support/Mosaic/. Nothing leaves the machine except
the messages you ask about, which go to Google Gemini for summarizing.

If something looks wrong
------------------------
Menu bar → Mosaic icon shows status. Quit from the menu and reopen.
To start over completely: quit the app, delete
~/Library/Application Support/Mosaic/, reopen, and re-pair. Also remove
the old device under WhatsApp → Linked Devices on your phone.
